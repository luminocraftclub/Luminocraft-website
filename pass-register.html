I understand that Lumino Craft's activities involve sensory play, arts and crafts, and interactive group activities, which may include use of child-safe materials, textured surfaces, water, and craft tools, and interaction with other children and caregivers.

I acknowledge that participation may involve certain inherent risks, such as:
  • skin contact with craft materials that may cause irritation or allergic reactions

I accept these ordinary risks on behalf of my child to the fullest extent permitted by law.

I understand that Lumino Craft aims to provide a calm, positive, and engaging experience. If a child becomes significantly distressed or overstimulated, Lumino Craft may suggest that the parent/guardian step outside briefly or end the session early if necessary. No refunds will be provided if a session ends early due to a child's distress or non-disclosure of significant needs.

Full credit is issued for cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance. Credits can be used for any future session with no expiry. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable but may be transferred to another family.

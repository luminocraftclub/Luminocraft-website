1. Parent / Guardian Attendance

A parent or legal guardian must remain on site and accompany the child for the entire session. This event is designed for babies and toddlers aged 0–4 years; unsupervised children will not be permitted to participate. I understand and agree to follow the above attendance expectations.

2. Health, Allergies & Special Needs

Please be aware of any allergies (including to food, paint, glue, latex, natural materials, etc.), medical conditions (e.g., skin sensitivities, respiratory conditions), sensory needs, or other information that may affect participation. I agree to inform Lumino Craft of any such needs in advance. I understand that Lumino Craft is not able to administer prescription medication beyond basic first aid measures unless agreed in writing.

3. Acknowledgement of Risks

I understand that Lumino Craft's activities involve sensory and arts and crafts play designed for babies and toddlers, which may include use of baby-safe paints, natural materials, textured surfaces, water, and soft craft tools, and interaction with other children and caregivers.

I acknowledge that participation may involve certain inherent risks, such as:
  • skin contact with craft materials that may cause irritation or allergic reactions
  • mouthing or contact with sensory materials (all materials used are non-toxic and baby-safe)
  • slips, trips, or falls
  • emotional upset or overstimulation in a group setting
I accept these ordinary risks on behalf of my child to the fullest extent permitted by law.

4. Behaviour & Session Management

I understand that Lumino Craft aims to provide a calm, positive, and sensory-friendly experience. If a child becomes significantly distressed or overstimulated, Lumino Craft may suggest that the parent/guardian step outside briefly or end the session early if necessary. No refunds will be provided if a session ends early due to a child's distress or non-disclosure of significant needs.

5. Illness & Cancellation

Children who are ill (fever, vomiting, diarrhea, contagious illness, or COVID-like symptoms) must not attend. Lumino Craft reserves the right to turn away any child who appears unwell on the day.

Full credit is issued for cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance. Credits can be used for any future session with no expiry. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable but may be transferred to another family.

6. Release & Waiver (to the extent permitted by law)

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, I voluntarily allow my child to participate in Lumino Craft programming and:
  • accept the ordinary risks described above; and
  • agree not to hold Lumino Craft (Play N Party Inc.), its owners, employees, contractors, volunteers, or event partners liable for minor injuries, losses, or damages arising from these ordinary risks, except where caused by gross negligence or wilful misconduct, or where liability cannot legally be waived.
I understand that in Canada, parental waivers for minors may have legal limits and may not fully prevent a child from bringing a future claim in all circumstances.

7. Acknowledgement

I have read, understood, and agree to the terms of this Parent/Guardian Waiver, Consent, and Media Release. I confirm that all information provided is accurate to the best of my knowledge.
